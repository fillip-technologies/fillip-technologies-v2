# Get a Quote Pricing Document

## Pricing Notes

- Currency: INR
- GST: 18%
- Package card prices are shown after tier discount and rounded to the nearest INR 500.
- Final quote totals are calculated from exact discounted values, then GST is added.
- Add-ons receive a flat 25% launch discount in the final quote calculation.
- Monthly services are marked as `/mo`.
- Ad spend is not included in Performance Marketing pricing.
